# How to ride Elron trains

You do not have to buy tickets beforehand: each train has a ticket selling person,
who can sell you a ticket based on the boarding and destination stations.

Alternatively visit https://elron.ee/ to buy a ticket in advance with a small discount.

In Summer, taking bikes in diesel trains costs extra. You can pay the charge both
online and to a ticket person.

Follow the website for disruptions: on Eastern directions some parts of the route
may be [served by buses](https://elron.ee/en/railway-repairs)
due to the ongoing electrification works.

See [this page](https://elron.ee/en/piletiinfo/piletid) for more info and prices
(published as PDF files).
